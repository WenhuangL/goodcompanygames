title: Privacy Policy for Tango
---

**Effective date:** 17 September 2026

This policy explains what Tango does with information about you. It applies to the Tango mobile
game on iOS and Android, published by Good Company Games ("we", "us").

Tango is intended for a general audience, which includes children. Because of that we treat
everyone as a child unless they tell us otherwise, and we ask for a year of birth before anything
else happens. That answer never leaves your device.

## What the game itself collects

Nothing that identifies you.

Tango has no sign-in, no name or email field, no leaderboards and no chat. It stores the
following on the device, using the operating system's own app storage:

- which levels you have finished, and how quickly
- your hint balance and when it last refilled
- whether you have completed today's daily puzzle
- the year of birth you entered on first launch
- a note of whether you have bought anything

Uninstalling the game removes all of it from the device.

**A copy of your level times is kept online**, so that reinstalling the game or changing phone
does not lose your progress. That copy contains level numbers and how many seconds each took, and
nothing else — no name, no email, no address book, nothing you have typed. It is stored against an
anonymous identifier created for the installation, which we cannot connect to you, to any other
app, or to advertising.

**We measure how the game is played**, but only for players aged 16 or over who have not turned it
off. See below.

## What advertising collects

Tango shows advertisements supplied by Google AdMob. Google's advertising software runs inside
the app and collects information for that purpose, including:

- device advertising identifiers, and general device information such as model and operating
  system version
- your IP address, from which an approximate (city-level) location can be derived
- whether an advertisement was shown, viewed or tapped

**If the year of birth entered on first launch indicates a child, or no answer is available,
Tango instructs Google to serve only non-personalised advertisements and to restrict content to
its most general rating.** Non-personalised advertisements still use coarse location and
information about the app itself in order to be shown at all, but they are not selected using a
profile of the person watching.

Google's own handling of this information is described at
<https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites> and
<https://business.safety.google/privacy/>.

If you are in the United Kingdom, the European Economic Area or Switzerland, you will be asked for
advertising consent the first time the game runs, before any advertisement is requested. You can
change that answer at any time from the Settings screen.

## What usage measurement collects

To find out which puzzles are too hard and whether the advertising gets in the way, the game
records events such as "level 12 started", "level 12 finished in 96 seconds with one hint" and "an
advertisement was shown". These carry level numbers, durations and counts. They carry nothing you
have typed and nothing that identifies you.

**This is switched off entirely for anyone under 16, and for anyone whose age we do not know.** It
can also be switched off by an adult at any time from the Settings screen, and doing so asks for
everything already collected to be deleted, not merely for collection to stop.

## What buying something involves

Purchases are handled entirely by the App Store or Google Play. We never see your card details,
billing address or payment information; the stores tell the game only that a purchase succeeded,
so it can unlock what you paid for. The stores' own privacy policies apply to that transaction.

## What we do not do

- We do not sell or share personal information, in the sense the California Consumer Privacy Act
  gives those words.
- We do not use your information to build a profile of you.
- We do not send you email, notifications or marketing.
- We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is described above.

## Your choices

- **Reset your advertising identifier**, or turn off personalised advertising entirely, in your
  device settings — iOS: *Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking*; Android: *Settings → Google
  → Ads*.
- **Remove advertising** with the one-off in-app purchase. After that no advertisement is
  requested and the advertising software makes no further requests.
- **Change your advertising consent** from the game's Settings screen, if you are in a region
  where consent is required.
- **Delete everything** by uninstalling the game.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what information is held about you, to
have it corrected or deleted, or to object to its use. Because Tango holds nothing about you on
our side, most such requests are best directed to Google, whose links are above. You are welcome
to write to us at goodcompanygamesco@gmail.com and we will help where we can.

If you are in the UK or EEA, the legal bases we rely on are your consent (for personalised
advertising) and legitimate interests (for showing non-personalised advertising, without which the
game could not be free).

## Children

Tango is not directed exclusively at children, but we expect children to play it. We do not
knowingly collect personal information from a child for any purpose other than showing a
non-personalised advertisement, as described above. If you believe a child has provided us with
personal information, write to goodcompanygamesco@gmail.com and we will delete it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at https://wenhuangl.github.io/goodcompanygames/privacy/ and the effective date
above will change. Material changes will be announced in the game.
